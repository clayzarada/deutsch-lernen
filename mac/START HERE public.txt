DEUTSCH LERNEN FOR MAC
======================
Made by Clay Zarada. © 2026 Zarada Holdings Group. All rights reserved.

A desktop app for learning German. Everything runs on your own Mac; your books and progress stay there.

INSTALL (about 5-10 minutes, once)

 1. Python: the app needs Python 3.10 or newer. If you don't have it, the installer opens the download page
    for you: get the macOS installer from python.org, install it, then do step 2 again.

 2. Double-click "build_mac.command" in this folder.
    If macOS says it can't be opened because it's from an unidentified developer: right-click it, choose
    Open, then Open again.
    A Terminal window shows the progress. When it says "Done!", the app opens by itself.

 3. Drag "Deutsch lernen" from your Applications folder to the Dock.

FIRST STEPS IN THE APP

 - Settings: pick English or Türkçe under "App language", then set your level, daily minutes and goal.
 - Settings: paste your Claude API key to turn on the AI features (practice sets, marking, flashcards, the
   coach). The download page explains how to get one, step by step. A Google Gemini key is optional and
   only needed for listening episodes and pronunciation.
 - Library: import a textbook PDF.
 - Today: press "Plan my day".
 - Declensions: start the journey on the first planet. This works without any API key.

Your data and keys are kept in ~/Library/Application Support/Deutsch lernen. Reinstalling or updating the
app never deletes them. You can delete this folder once the app is installed.
